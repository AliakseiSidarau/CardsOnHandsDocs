CardsOnHand Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

CardsOnHand ("the App", "we", "us") is an app for storing and using loyalty cards and their barcodes. It is available for iPhone and iPad (iOS) and for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App stores on your device, what usage information is collected for analytics, how in-app purchases work, which third parties are involved, and what choices and rights you have. Where the two versions behave differently, this is stated explicitly.

In short
  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your cards, barcodes, notes and photos stay on your device. We have no server that stores them.
  • We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to collect anonymous usage statistics. We never send card names, barcode numbers, notes, photos or search text. On iOS, you can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share Usage Statistics.
  • We do not show ads, do not use the advertising identifier, do not track you across other apps or websites and do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • On iOS, the Premium purchase is processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

1. Who is responsible

The App is developed and published by the developer of CardsOnHand, who is the controller of the information described in this policy. You can reach us at pay.qoqo.s.pal@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

To provide its features, the App stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Card names, notes, colors and backgrounds.
  • Barcode values and barcode formats, and generated barcode images (cache).
  • Card photos that you take or choose.
  • Favorites, the "Show on widget" setting (iOS) and sort and display preferences.
  • App settings, such as theme, language and screen brightness for barcodes.
  • On iOS: whether Premium is unlocked, so the App works offline.

This information is not sent to us or to the analytics service, and we do not operate a server that stores your loyalty cards. It is protected by your operating system's app sandbox and, on iOS, by Data Protection while your device is locked.

Device backups. Like most apps, the App's local data may be included in backups that your operating system makes, depending on your settings: iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iOS, and Android Backup (Google) on Android. These backups are managed by Apple or Google under their own terms, not by us.

3. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how the App is used: which features are popular, where users run into problems and whether free limits and Premium work as intended. We use this information only to maintain and improve the App. You can stop analytics collection at any time (see section 12).

When you use a feature, the App logs an event with a few technical parameters, such as counts, barcode formats and on/off values. The events fall into these groups:

The iOS version also sets four user properties: whether Premium is active, the number of cards as a range (for example "6-10"), the App language and the theme.

3.2 Information collected automatically by Firebase

Firebase Analytics also collects some information automatically, such as an app instance identifier created by Firebase (and, on iOS, the identifier for vendor), the device model, the operating system and App version, the language and region, and events such as the first launch, sessions, engagement time and App updates. Google may use your IP address to determine an approximate country or region. See Google's description of automatically collected events.

3.3 What we never collect

  • Card names, barcode values, notes, card photos or other contents of your cards.
  • Search text.
  • Your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • Your contacts, photo library or files, apart from the photos and backup files you choose.

The App also filters these values out of analytics events in its code, as an extra safeguard.

3.4 No advertising and no tracking

The App contains no ads. Collection of the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) and ad personalization signals are turned off. On iOS, the App uses a version of Firebase Analytics that does not include advertising identifier code. Analytics data is not linked to your identity, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites. This is why the iOS App does not ask for tracking permission.

3.5 Legal basis, retention and transfers

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process analytics data on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning analytics off as described in section 12. Google processes the data on our behalf as a data processor. Google Analytics keeps user-level and event-level data for the retention period set in our Google Analytics settings (by default 2 months), and aggregated reports for longer. Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Such transfers are covered by Google's data processing terms, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. For more information, see the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases (iOS)

On iOS, you can unlock Premium with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information. The App receives a confirmation of the purchase from Apple, which it uses to unlock Premium and stores on your device. "Restore Purchases" asks Apple to confirm earlier purchases. Purchase-related analytics events are described in section 3.1. Apple's handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Camera, photos and barcode recognition

  • Camera. The App asks for camera access to scan barcodes and to take card photos. The camera is used only while the scanner or photo screen is open.
  • Photos. You can choose an image to read a barcode from it or to use as a card photo. On iOS, images are chosen through the system photo picker, so the App only sees the photo you pick and not your library.
  • On-device recognition. Barcodes are recognized on your device. On iOS this uses Apple's frameworks; on Android, Google ML Kit. Images and barcode values are not uploaded. Google ML Kit may send limited diagnostic information, such as performance metrics and device and app information, to Google under the ML Kit terms.

You can allow or deny these permissions in your device settings: on iOS in Settings > CardsOnHand, on Android in Settings > Apps > CardsOnHand > Permissions. If you deny a permission, only the related feature stops working.

6. Home Screen widget and quick actions (iOS)

If you add the CardsOnHand widget, the App copies the names and colors of the cards shown in the widget to a storage area on your device that the App shares with its widget. This information does not leave your device. The widget shows card names on your Home Screen, where anyone who can see your screen can read them. Home Screen quick actions only open the App and do not collect any information.

7. Import and export

You can export your cards to a backup file (.cohbackup) and import them again, including between the iOS and Android versions. A backup file contains card names, barcode values and formats, notes, colors and favorite and widget settings. Card photos are not included. The file is created only when you ask for it, and you decide where it is saved and with whom it is shared, for example in Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, by email or with AirDrop. Please keep backup files safe because they contain your loyalty card information.

8. Contacting us

If you use "Contact developer" in the App, your email app opens a message to us. We only receive your email address and message if you send it. We use them only to answer you and keep them only as long as needed for that.

9. Who we share information with

  • Google (Firebase Analytics) receives the analytics information described in section 3, as our data processor.
  • Apple processes in-app purchases on iOS (section 4).
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device. Delete individual cards in the App, or delete all App data by uninstalling the App. On iOS, "Offload App" keeps your data, so use "Delete App" to remove it. On Android, you can also use Settings > Apps > CardsOnHand > Storage > Clear storage.
  • Backup files that you exported are not deleted together with the App. Delete them where you saved them.
  • Analytics data is deleted automatically after the retention period described in section 3.5. On iOS, turning off "Share Usage Statistics" stops collection and deletes the analytics identifier stored on your device, so later data can no longer be linked to earlier data. Deleting the App also stops all further collection. Analytics data is not linked to your identity, so we can only find data about your installation if you provide identifying details. If you contact us, we will help you as far as we are able to.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and the UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive your data in a portable format (the App's export feature already gives you a copy of your cards) and to withdraw consent. You can object to analytics directly in the App (section 12). To exercise your other rights, email us at pay.qoqo.s.pal@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. How to stop analytics collection

  • iOS: open the App and go to Settings > Privacy and turn off "Share Usage Statistics". Collection stops immediately and the analytics identifier on your device is deleted. You can turn it back on at any time. The same screen links to this policy.
  • Android: uninstall the App to stop all collection.
  • Both versions: you can also email us to object to analytics processing or to request deletion of the data related to your installation, as described in sections 10 and 11.

13. Children

CardsOnHand is a general-purpose app and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

14. Security

Your cards are stored only on your device, inside the App's sandbox. Analytics data is sent to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Please protect your device with a passcode or biometrics and keep exported backup files secure.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or legal requirements change. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a change significantly affects how your information is handled, we will also point it out in the App or in the App's release notes.
